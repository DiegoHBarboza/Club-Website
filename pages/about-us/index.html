About Us

At SPORTME, we believe sport is for everyone. As a community-driven sports club, we’re proud to bring people of all ages and abilities together through an exciting and diverse range of activities. Whether you’re looking to sharpen your skills, try something new, or simply stay active while having fun, we’ve got something for you. From the fast pace of basketball and the skill of camogie, to the adrenaline of skateboarding and American football, our club embraces both tradition and innovation in sport. For those who want to combine movement, rhythm, and culture, we offer capoeira, while our karate classes provide discipline, focus, and strength. Our mission is simple: to create a welcoming, supportive environment where everyone can thrive, regardless of experience level. With passionate coaches, inclusive programs, and a strong sense of community, SPORTME is more than just a sports club—it’s a place to connect, grow, and achieve your personal best. Come join us, challenge yourself, and be part of a team that celebrates diversity, energy, and the joy of sport.
